# AI use — policy and candidate log

AI assistance is welcome for discovery, design critique, code, fixture generation,
and tests. You must understand the submitted work and verify provider facts
against approved source evidence. AI output is never itself evidence of a code
meaning, a field's unit, an endpoint, or distribution permission.

Do not upload restricted provider specifications to an external AI service unless
the organizer has explicitly approved that service and use. Use approved tools
or summarize nonrestricted design questions. Never include credentials or real
employee data. Record meaningful interactions; exhaustive transcripts are not required.
